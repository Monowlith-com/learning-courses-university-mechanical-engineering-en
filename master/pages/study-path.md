# Study path {#studienweg}

## Foundations {#S1}

Begin with mathematics and statics. Attempt the exercises before opening the solutions. The bachelor core comprises M1–M10; M11 advances methods, M12–M15 are electives, and M16 connects practice and research. Study M16-L1 to L4 after the bachelor core and M16-L5 after M11 and one elective. M16-L6 accompanies both projects. All lessons are available in draft form.

## Study areas {#S2}

- M1 – Engineering mathematics and modelling (B)
- M2 – Statics and mechanics of materials (B)
- M3 – Kinematics and dynamics (B)
- M4 – Materials and material behaviour (B)
- M5 – Thermodynamics (B)
- M6 – Fluid mechanics and heat transfer (B)
- M7 – Engineering design and CAD (B)
- M8 – Machine elements (B)
- M9 – Manufacturing and production (B)
- M10 – Measurement and mechatronics (B)
- M11 – Simulation and numerical methods (M)
- M12 – Advanced design and materials (W)
- M13 – Advanced energy and process engineering (W)
- M14 – Advanced manufacturing and digitalisation (W)
- M15 – Advanced application fields (W)
- M16 – Engineering practice and research project (P)
