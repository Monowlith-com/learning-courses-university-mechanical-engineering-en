# Course overview – Mechanical Engineering {#kurseinstieg}

Private draft U_MEE. Available: 85 of 85 planned lessons. This self-study course is not an accredited degree programme.

## Audience and objectives {#S1}

For prospective students and independent learners with school mathematics and physics. The planned study path covers modelling, design, energy, manufacturing, simulation and scientific work.
